# 01 — SKY JAIL

**Theme:** Patience beats power. A spawn-camper who thinks bricks are a weapon gets
out-engineered and mailed to the skybox.
**Game / Setting:** Classic Baseplate — an unrestricted **Building Tools by F3X**
sandbox server. Flat grey baseplate, default spawn, open sky.
**Troll:** `xX_Destroyer_Xx` — has anchored a wall across spawn and slides a red
"LAVA" brick into anyone who joins.
**Runtime:** ~1:55
**Hashtags:** #roblox #f3x #trollrevenge #robloxedit #fyp #robloxbuilding

---

## THE SETUP — 0:00–0:30

**0:00–0:08 · Visual**
Hard open: our avatar finishes the spawn animation already boxed in by a huge grey
F3X wall. A red brick labeled `LAVA` slides in from the left and touches us — death,
ragdoll. Respawn. Same wall. Same brick. Death. Chat spams: `L + ratio`,
`cant build noob`, `day4 😂`.

**0:08–0:18 · Visual**
Third respawn. We jump, we clip the wall, we can't get over. Camera pans up the wall
— it's 40 studs tall. Troll's avatar is visible on top, crouch-spamming.

**0:18–0:30 · Visual**
We equip the F3X tool. The toolbar slides up on screen. We look at it, then look at
the troll. Slow zoom on our avatar's face.

**Audio / SFX**
- Phonk bed fades in at 0:03, low (−18 dB).
- **Record scratch** the instant the wall first fills frame (0:01).
- Classic **OOF** stacked on each of the 3 deaths.
- Chat-spam "pop" tick per message.
- Music ducks to near-silent at 0:26 for the toolbar reveal — one clean synth note.

**Editing notes**
- NO intro, NO logo. Frame 1 = trapped.
- Speed-ramp the 3 deaths: 1.4× on the walk-in, snap to 1.0× on each impact, hard
  cut on black (2 frames) between respawns.
- Punch-in zoom (scale 1.0→1.3 over 6 frames) on the chat spam at 0:12.
- Text pop, top third: **"day 4 of this guy 💀"** (0:05), then **"ok. new plan."**
  (0:27).
- 0:18 toolbar reveal: desaturate everything except the F3X toolbar (quick mask) for
  0:6 s. Small arrow + label: **"Building Tools by F3X"**.
- Loop-bait anchor: memorize this spawn framing — the last shot returns here.

**TTS (Brian, dry)**
> "This kid has spawn-trapped me for four days. Today I finally opened the build menu."

## THE TROLL ATTACK — 0:30–0:50

**0:30–0:40 · Visual**
Troll drops off the wall, sees our tool is out, laughs (jump-spam). He grabs a 2×2
brick with F3X Move, clones a wobbly tower straight down onto our head, burying us.

**0:40–0:50 · Visual**
He resizes a thin brick into a long "piston", rams it into us — we get flung across
the baseplate, tumbling. He teabags at the edge of frame. Chat: `stay down`.

**Audio / SFX**
- Cartoon **"boing"** per brick he stacks.
- **Vine boom** on the piston fling (0:44).
- Laugh track low under the jump-spam.
- Wind-whoosh as we sail through the air.

**Editing notes**
- Screen shake (4 px, 3 Hz) for 0:5 s on the fling impact.
- At the apex of the fling: slow-mo to 0.3×, **freeze-frame**, red circle + arrow on
  the troll, text: **"remember this guy →"**. Hold 0:8 s, then snap back to 1.0×.
- Emoji rain: 🤡 ×6 falling, 0:47–0:50.
- Keep cuts ~0.6 s here — busy, but not yet the montage pace.

**TTS (Brian)**
> "Cool. Laugh it up. You have no idea what's in this toolbar."

## THE BIG BRAIN REVENGE — 0:50–1:30

The troll goes AFK-typing in chat. We work fast and quiet.

**0:50–0:58 · Visual — BOX**
F3X **Move** tool. Grab one 4×4 neon brick. Ctrl-drag to clone it into a wall.
Clone again — floor, ceiling, four sides — a hollow cube sealing the troll inside.
He's still typing, doesn't notice.

**0:58–1:06 · Visual — SEAL**
F3X **Resize** tool. Stretch each panel a few studs so every seam overlaps. No gaps.
Troll finishes typing, turns around, walks into a wall. Starts punching it.

**1:06–1:14 · Visual — ANCHOR**
F3X **Material** tool → set cube to black Neon. F3X **Anchor** tool → click every
panel. Now he can't shove it. Inside-cube POV cut: troll spinning, trapped.

**1:14–1:24 · Visual — ENGINE**
Under the cube we stack four anchored bricks, then attach F3X **Rocket** surfaces to
the bottom panel. F3X **Weld** tool: link cube ↔ engine block into one assembly.

**1:24–1:30 · Visual — ARM**
F3X **Anchor** tool again — un-anchor *only* the cube assembly. We back away. We type
in chat: `3` … `2` … `1`.

**Audio / SFX**
- Satisfying ASMR **"tick"** synced to every clone placement and every anchor click.
- Rising synth riser building the whole 40 s.
- Music swells back in at 0:58, climbing.
- On-screen countdown: **Minecraft "click"** per number.

**Editing notes**
- This is the retention core. Cut on **every** tool action — ~0.4 s per shot.
- Timelapse the box build (2.5×) with the ticks landing exactly on cuts.
- Persistent UI overlay, bottom-left: **`BUILDING SKY JAIL ▓▓▓▓▓░░ 70%`** filling as
  we go.
- Section stingers as text: **"1. BOX"**, **"2. SEAL"**, **"3. ANCHOR"**,
  **"4. ENGINE"** — punch in on the beat.
- Then the pattern-break: hold ONE locked wide shot for the last 4 s (1:26–1:30).
  Stillness after the chaos = the tension spike before the payoff.

**TTS (Brian)**
> "Box him in. Seal the seams. Anchor it so he can't push out. Then — physics."

## THE CLIMAX & OUTRO — 1:30–2:00

**1:30–1:38 · Visual**
On `1`, the cube ERUPTS upward on a pillar of fire, straight out of frame. Whip-pan
up to follow it — the black cube shrinks into the skybox, baseplate falling away
below.

**1:38–1:46 · Visual**
Cut to the troll's POV: stuck in the neon cube, spinning slowly, the world a tiny
grey square far below. Beat of silence. His avatar goes limp — `xX_Destroyer_Xx has
left the game`.

**1:46–1:54 · Visual**
Cut to our avatar walking through the now-open, clear spawn. We place one small brick
with the F3X **Text** tool. It reads **`GG`**. Server chat: `LMAOOO`, `W`, `king`,
`he's still going up`.

**1:54–2:00 · Visual**
Slow push-in on the `GG` brick. Freeze. Film grain + slight zoom. Sticker:
**"SUBSCRIBE for day 5 👑"**.

**Audio / SFX**
- Huge **whoosh + boom** on launch (1:30).
- Wind howl through the skybox POV, then a hard 0:5 s of total silence before "left
  the game".
- **OOF** on the disconnect.
- Phonk bed slams back in at full on the **`GG`** reveal (1:47).
- "W" ding + short applause bed under the end sticker.

**Editing notes**
- Split-screen 1:40–1:44: LEFT = troll spinning in the void, RIGHT = us calm at
  spawn. Then collapse to full-frame on us.
- Launch shot: camera locked, let him leave frame — don't chase with a zoom, the
  empty sky sells it.
- **Loop-bait:** final framing on the `GG` brick matches the 0:00 spawn framing, so
  the loop restarts feeling like "day 5".

**TTS (Brian, flat)**
> "Four days. One rocket. Worth it. He's probably still going up."

### Why it retains
The 40-second build montage is a visible progress bar the viewer wants to see fill —
they stay for the launch. The mid-fling freeze-frame ("remember this guy") plants a
debt that the skybox POV pays off. The 4-second silent hold right before 1:30 is the
single most important cut: it stops the scroll thumb.
