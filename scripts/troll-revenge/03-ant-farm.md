# 03 — ANT FARM

**Theme:** Whoever reaches the command bar second still wins. An admin-abuser gets
shrunk to 0.2 scale and stood on.
**Game / Setting:** a **Kohl's Admin House / HD Admin** showcase server. Two admin
"giver" pads on the map; the troll got to the first one.
**Troll:** `ThanosMainXD` — spamming `;fling all`, `;fire`, spawned 100 noob NPCs,
standing on a pedestal like a throne.
**Runtime:** ~1:57
**Hashtags:** #roblox #admincommands #trollrevenge #hdadmin #robloxedit #fyp

---

## THE SETUP — 0:00–0:30

**0:00–0:10 · Visual**
Open on maximum chaos: avatars flying in every direction, fire across the floor, ~100
noob NPCs clogging the map, the troll tiny on his pedestal spamming. Green
`[SERVER]` command confirmations flood the chat. We get flung into the skybox and
land face-first.

**0:10–0:20 · Visual**
We get up. Flung again before we take two steps. And again. Each time we crawl a
little closer to the **second admin pad** glowing on the far side of the map.

**0:20–0:30 · Visual**
Close on our avatar's face, then a slow tilt up to the troll on his pedestal,
backlit, teabagging. He looks huge from here.

**Audio / SFX**
- Hard distorted phonk drop from frame 1.
- **OOF** machine-gunned on every fling and landing (stack 4–5).
- Fire crackle bed, a dry **"bruh"** on the worst landing (0:08).

**Editing notes**
- Start on the single most unhinged frame you can find.
- RGB-split / datamosh glitch on each fling (2–3 frames).
- Cuts at ~0.3 s through the whole setup.
- Text: **"this server needs an exorcism"** (0:03). Red arrow on the pedestal:
  **"the problem"** (0:24).
- Low-angle hero shot of the troll at 0:27 — deliberately make him look powerful.
  The payoff is inverting this exact shot.

**TTS (Adam, deep, ominous)**
> "One kid. All the power. Time to take it back."

---

## THE TROLL ATTACK — 0:30–0:50

**0:30–0:40 · Visual**
Troll sees us almost at the second pad. He types `;freeze ThanosMainXD` → wait, no —
he types `;freeze [our name]`. We lock up two studs from the pad, mid-stride.

**0:40–0:50 · Visual**
He walks over, types `;size ThanosMainXD 5` — inflates to five times height, towering.
He teabags on our frozen head, then `;fling [our name]`. We ragdoll away. Respawn,
sprint back.

**Audio / SFX**
- **Ice-crack** SFX on the freeze (0:33), isolated, music ducks.
- Deep **footstep booms** as the sized-up troll walks (screen shake per step).
- "womp womp" trombone on the freeze.
- Laugh track under the teabag.

**Editing notes**
- Slow-mo (0.3×) on the frozen reach for the pad — *so close*, fingertips almost on
  it. Freeze-frame, **"😤"**, text: **"he's about to regret this"**.
- Keep the low, wide hero angle on the giant troll — bank it.
- Snap back to real-time on the fling with a **Vine boom**.

**TTS (Adam)**
> "He froze me one step away. I only need one step."

---

## THE BIG BRAIN REVENGE — 0:50–1:30

We reach the pad. Every command is typed on screen, then we hard-cut to its result.

| # | Command (typed on screen) | Result on screen |
|---|---------------------------|------------------|
| 1 | `;unfreeze me` | we break loose, ice shatters off |
| 2 | `;god me` | brief golden shimmer — invincible |
| 3 | `;tp me ThanosMainXD` | we blink in directly behind him |
| 4 | `;freeze ThanosMainXD` | he locks up mid-teabag |
| 5 | `;size ThanosMainXD 0.2` | he shrinks to an ant. comedy beat — hold 0:8 s |
| 6 | `;size me 10` | we rise into a giant, camera tilts up to reveal |
| 7 | `;fire ThanosMainXD` + `;sparkles ThanosMainXD` | tiny troll on fire, sparkling |
| 8 | *(walk over, stand on him)* → `;fling ThanosMainXD` | the ant launches off-screen |
| 9 | `;clear` + `;unfreeze all` | fire gone, 100 noobs gone, players unfrozen |

Server chat erupts: `THANK YOU`, `MVP`, `finally`, `W`.

**Audio / SFX**
- Each command: a crisp **"type + enter" click**, then the HD Admin **confirm ding**.
- `;size 0.2` → **descending slide-whistle**.
- `;size me 10` → **ascending power-up swell**.
- Giant footsteps = deep booms.
- Final `;fling` → **Vine boom + OOF**.
- `;clear` → a big whoosh as everything vanishes.

**Editing notes**
- Retention core. Two-shot rhythm per command: (a) tight close-up of the chat bar
  typing it, (b) hard cut to the result. ~0.5 s each, locked to the music's kick.
- On `;size 0.2`: whip-zoom DOWN to the ant.
- On `;size me 10`: dolly back + tilt up to reveal the giant — this is the inversion
  of the 0:27 hero shot, same framing, roles flipped.
- Fighting-game **`COMBO ×7`** counter in the corner, ticking up per command.
- Escalating screen shake, biggest on the fling.
- **"POV:"** text sticker per beat ("POV: you froze the wrong guy").

**TTS (Adam)**
> "Unfreeze. God mode. Teleport. Now we resize the problem."

## THE CLIMAX & OUTRO — 1:30–2:00

**1:30–1:40 · Visual**
Giant avatar scoops the ant-troll onto our palm (`;tp` him to us mid-air), walks to
the edge of the baseplate, and types `;jail ThanosMainXD` — a cage snaps around him,
ant-sized.

**1:40–1:48 · Visual**
We set the tiny jail back on the pedestal where he started — still shrunk, still
sparkling, still caged. We type `;size me 1`, back to normal, and dust our hands.

**1:48–1:55 · Visual**
We walk away from camera, not looking back. Particle burst behind us from the
`;clear`. `ThanosMainXD has left the game`.

**1:55–2:00 · Visual**
Final frame: the pedestal, now holding one thumbtack-sized jail. Our avatar's back,
walking out. Chat: `balance restored`, `W`. Freeze, grain, chromatic aberration.
Sticker: **"sub for more admin wars ⚔️"**.

**Audio / SFX**
- Music strips to a bare kick for the `;jail` **"clang"** (1:38).
- Full phonk drop returns on the walk-away (1:48).
- `left the game` → 0:5 s of wind and nothing. Let it breathe.

**Editing notes**
- Slow-mo hero walk (0.5×) away from camera. **Do not** cut back to the troll.
- Text: **"GG. rebalanced."** (1:52).
- **Loop-bait:** last frame (pedestal + tiny jail, our back turned) rhymes with the
  first-act hero shot of the troll on the same pedestal. The throne changed hands;
  the loop makes you re-watch to see how.

**TTS (Adam)**
> "Same throne. New king. And he's the size of a thumbtack. Don't grief servers."

---

### Why it retains
The command table is a call-and-response the brain wants to complete — nine little
"what happens next" beats in 40 seconds. The 0:27 low hero shot of the giant troll is
a setup the audience doesn't know is a setup; re-using the identical framing at 1:20
with the roles flipped is the payoff that earns the re-watch. The `;size 0.2` comedy
hold is the frame people screenshot.
