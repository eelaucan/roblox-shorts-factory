# 02 — LANDLORD FROM HELL

**Theme:** His house, his rules — until the owner menu changes hands. A roommate who
abuses the house settings gets locked inside his own build and evicted by roleplay
cops.
**Game / Setting:** **Brookhaven 🏡RP**. Our player is the **Baby** role (tiny
hitbox, hard to hit, fits through gaps). Suburban house, driveway, the lake, the
police station.
**Troll:** `bacon_hair_47`, playing an adult roommate. Steals our sports car, drowns
it in the lake, blasts the house radio at 3 AM, locks us out.
**Runtime:** ~1:58
**Hashtags:** #brookhaven #roblox #trollrevenge #brookhavenrp #robloxedit #fyp

---

## THE SETUP — 0:00–0:30

**0:00–0:09 · Visual**
Cold open inside the house at "night": every light strobing, the radio speaker prop
visibly pulsing, bass distorting. Our Baby avatar is stuck in a crib in the corner.

**0:09–0:18 · Visual**
Troll sprints out, jumps into our sports car in the driveway, honks four times,
reverses over the mailbox, peels to the lake and drives straight in. Car bobs, sinks.
He swims back.

**0:18–0:30 · Visual**
Troll walks inside, turns to the door, and the padlock 🔒 icon appears — locked from
the inside. Through the window we see him lie down in *our* bed. Baby avatar rattles
the crib bars.

**Audio / SFX**
- Loud, clipping generic-EDM radio bed from frame 1 (diegetic — it's the in-game
  music player).
- Car-horn spam, tires screech, a big **splash** on the lake.
- **Door-lock "click"** at 0:20, isolated.
- Baby "goo goo" voiceline at 0:29.

**Editing notes**
- Open on the strobe + noise. Text, immediately: **"my roommate at 3AM:"** (0:01).
- **Flashing-lights warning sticker** top-right for the whole strobe stretch.
- Punch-in + **Vine boom** on the car hitting the lake (0:15).
- Cuts synced to the four horn honks (0:11–0:12) — one cut per honk.
- ❌🔇 emoji slapped on the pulsing speaker (0:05).

**TTS (Sarah, sarcastic)**
> "Perfect roommate. Steals my car, drowns it, locks me out. And I'm the baby."

## THE TROLL ATTACK — 0:30–0:50

**0:30–0:40 · Visual**
Troll opens the **House Menu**. He deletes our bedroom furniture piece by piece, then
swaps the whole house to the smallest build template. Our crib ends up in the
basement.

**0:40–0:50 · Visual**
He spam-places the siren/alarm prop around the house — six of them — sets every light
to the party/strobe setting, then drives off in the **ice cream truck**, jingle
blaring. Chat: `cope baby`, keyboard smash.

**Audio / SFX**
- Layered sirens stacking, each one louder.
- Ice-cream-truck jingle, pitched down ~20% (uncanny).
- Menu "select" blip per furniture deletion.

**Editing notes**
- Deliberate overload: 4 stacked quick-zooms, 6 px shake, red vignette pulsing, text
  **"🚨 CHAOS 🚨"**.
- Then — **hard cut to black + full silence for 0:5 s** at 0:49. Pattern interrupt.
- Break the silence with a single dry line.

**TTS (Sarah, calm now)**
> "Okay. He opened the house settings. That was the mistake."

## THE BIG BRAIN REVENGE — 0:50–1:30

Pure Brookhaven mechanics. No admin, no tools — just the owner menu and a small
hitbox.

**0:50–0:58 · Visual — THE SWAP**
Baby avatar waits pressed against the front door. The moment the troll walks IN, we
slip OUT through the gap (small hitbox). Outside, we open the **House Menu** — as the
owner — and set the door to **Locked**, owner-only.

**0:58–1:06 · Visual — STRIP THE ROOM**
From outside, through the menu, we delete every piece of furniture. Nothing left to
interact with. Troll runs door to door — all locked.

**1:06–1:14 · Visual — PIN**
We spam-place the "large sofa" prop into the room he's in, stacking them until he's
crushed into the corner, camera clipping.

**1:14–1:22 · Visual — HIS OWN MEDICINE**
We set every interior light to strobe and crank the house radio to *his* 3 AM song.
Now it's playing at him.

**1:22–1:30 · Visual — GLITCH HIM**
We spam the house-template swap — the interior flickers between layouts, and the
troll keeps falling through the floor as geometry reloads. We leave, get in the ice
cream truck.

**Audio / SFX**
- Crisp menu blip per action — cut rhythm rides these.
- The troll's song swelling back up at 1:16 — same track, now it's justice.
- Muffled troll rage "through the walls" (low-pass, quiet).
- Comedic wobble/boing on each house restyle.

**Editing notes**
- Screen-in-screen: main frame = our Baby calmly tapping the menu; corner inset =
  troll losing it inside.
- Cut on the menu blips, ~0.5 s.
- Speed-ramp the template spam (1:22–1:30) into a strobe of layouts — keep the
  flashing-lights sticker up.
- Overlay, bottom-left: **`EVICTION IN PROGRESS ▓▓▓▓▓░ 80%`**.
- Text stingers: **"1. lock him in"**, **"2. strip the room"**, **"3. his song now"**.

**TTS (Sarah)**
> "His house, his rules. Now it's my house — and rule one is he doesn't leave."

## THE CLIMAX & OUTRO — 1:30–2:00

**1:30–1:40 · Visual**
We drive the ice cream truck to the police station and lead two roleplay "cops" back
to the house, sirens on, a little convoy.

**1:40–1:48 · Visual**
As the cops reach the porch, we hit **Unlock** on the owner menu at the exact right
moment — the door opens, the troll bolts out at full sprint and runs straight into
the cops. Cuffed, walked to the Brookhaven jail cell.

**1:48–1:55 · Visual**
We park the ice cream truck directly on the troll's spawn point, so he respawns stuck
inside the truck. Then we drive to the lake and tow our sports car out with the tow
truck.

**1:55–2:00 · Visual**
Final shot: Baby avatar sitting on the roof of the recovered sports car in the
driveway. Behind, tiny in frame, the troll rattling the jail bars. Chat: `baby
supremacy`, `W landlord`. Push-in, grain. Sticker: **"part 2? 🍼"**.

**Audio / SFX**
- Ice-cream jingle, now triumphant / sped back to normal.
- **Jail-door "clang"** on the arrest.
- The troll's radio song **hard-cuts to silence** the instant the cell closes —
  the catharsis is the absence of the noise.
- Final Baby "goo goo ga ga" as a mic drop over the last frame.

**Editing notes**
- The unlock beat gets slow-mo (0.4×) + text **"⏱️ perfectly timed"**.
- Arrest = freeze-frame, zoom, **"🚔 EVICTED"** stamp.
- **Loop-bait:** first frame and last frame both feature the house radio speaker —
  start = blasting, end = dead silent. The loop restarts on the noise.

**TTS (Sarah)**
> "Evicted, arrested, and his getaway truck is now his cell. Baby wins."

### Why it retains
No god-tools — every move is something the viewer could do in Brookhaven tonight,
which makes it feel *achievable* and shareable ("bro do this to your friend"). The
0:5 s cut-to-black at 0:49 resets attention right before the payoff. The
perfectly-timed door unlock is the single shareable "clip within the clip".
