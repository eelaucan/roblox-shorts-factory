# A4 — MOM CAN YOU SEND ME 4 ROBUX 🤑 (LYRIC PRANK)

**Format:** Lyric prank — text a stranger song lyrics line-by-line, film the reaction.
**Game / Setting:** Brookhaven or any public hangout with an active random player.
**Cast:** YOU · a RANDOM PLAYER (unscripted).
**Runtime:** ~25 s
**Title options:** "Mom can you send me 4 robux 🤑 (lyric prank)" · "Texting a stranger lyrics 😭" · "His reaction had me DYING 💀"
**Hashtags:** #roblox #lyricprank #brookhaven #robloxedit #fyp

> **Copyright note:** rewrite the trend's lyric into an original Roblox parody (as
> below) and use the platform's licensed-audio picker for the backing track on
> export — don't drop the copyrighted master into a monetised upload.

---

## 0:00 – 0:03 · HOOK
- **Visual:** Split — your chat box on one side, a random player standing in Brookhaven on the other.
- **Text:** `texting a stranger song lyrics 😭`
- **Audio:** The trending song fades in low so viewers recognise the melody.
- **Editing:** Start on the player already in frame. No setup.

## 0:03 – 0:19 · THE LINES
Deliver one parody lyric per beat, cut to the reaction after each:

| You type | Their reaction |
|----------|----------------|
| `mom… can you send me 4 robux` | player stops walking, turns |
| `i'm at the catalog with my friends` | types `what` |
| `and i'm gonna buy the dominus` | `thinking` emote, `bro?` |
| `no i will NOT calm down` | starts walking away |
| `mom. the limited is RESELLING` | `??? 💀` |

- **Audio:** Song continues under. **Oof** or a light laugh track on the best reaction beat only.
- **Editing:** Show *your* lines as big on-screen text bubbles (not tiny Roblox chat). Hard cut to the player's reply each time, zoomed in on their character + chat.

## 0:19 – 0:25 · PAYOFF
- **Visual:** Final parody line lands. The player fires back something unintentionally funny — e.g. `bro I'm not your mom 💀` — and leaves.
- **Text:** freeze their message, then `he said WHAT 😭 pt 2?`
- **Audio:** Song hits its recognisable drop right here; **Vine boom** on the freeze.
- **Editing:** Freeze-frame on their last message, grain, zoom. **Loop-bait:** end back on the split-screen layout from 0:00.

---

### Why it works
Viewers who know the song get an in-group payoff and comment the title ("what song
is this" / "he did NOT say that") — both strong ranking signals. Real, unscripted
stranger reactions read as authentic in a feed full of staged skits, and **"pt 2?"**
banks the follow. Keep the lines clean and Roblox-specific so it's screenshot-safe.
