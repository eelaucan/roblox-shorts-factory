# A3 — WHICH BACON DO YOU SAVE?! 😥💔

**Format:** "Choose One" dilemma — binary like-vs-comment vote with a hard cliffhanger.
**Game / Setting:** a small built lava-pit set (Roblox Studio, or a Natural Disaster
Survival lava round). Two ledges, symmetrical, a lever between them.
**Cast:** BACON-LEFT · BACON-RIGHT (both default avatars) · YOU at the lever.
**Runtime:** ~20 s
**Title options:** "Which Bacon do you save?! 😥💔" · "You can only pick ONE 😭" · "This choice is impossible 💔"
**Hashtags:** #roblox #baconhair #robloxedit #chooseone #fyp

---

## 0:00 – 0:03 · HOOK
- **Visual:** Wide, perfectly symmetrical: two Bacon Hairs dangling by their fingertips from two ledges over a glowing lava pit. A lever dead-centre.
- **Text:** `you can only save ONE 😭`
- **Audio:** Tense strings + a low heartbeat. No meme SFX yet.
- **Editing:** Hold the symmetrical wide 2 s. Slow zoom toward the lever.

## 0:03 – 0:09 · MEET THEM
- **Visual:** Cut to BACON-LEFT — `please` / begging emote, straining. Cut to BACON-RIGHT — looking down at the lava, then up, `cry` emote.
- **Text:** Left bubble `it's my first day 🥹` · Right bubble `my little brother is watching 😢`
- **Audio:** Strings rise. Heartbeat speeds up. A small rock tumbles into the lava with a hiss.
- **Editing:** Cut left/right/left/right, ~0.7 s each, tightening. Zoom on each face.

## 0:09 – 0:15 · THE VOTE
- **Visual:** YOU stand at the lever, hand on it, head turning between the two. Two glowing prompts appear over each Bacon.
- **Text:** `❤️ LIKE = save the left one` / `💬 COMMENT = save the right one` · then a countdown `5… 4… 3…`
- **Audio:** Ticking clock layered under the strings. Heartbeat loudest here.
- **Editing:** Split the frame in two with the like/comment labels locked in place. Countdown numbers punch in on screen.

## 0:15 – 0:20 · CLIFFHANGER
- **Visual:** Freeze the instant before YOU pull the lever — both Bacons still hanging, your hand mid-motion.
- **Text:** `the votes decide. pt 2 tomorrow 👀`
- **Audio:** Everything cuts to silence, then a single **Vine boom** on the freeze.
- **Editing:** Hard freeze-frame, grain, slight zoom. Do **not** resolve it. **Loop-bait:** the frozen frame matches the 0:00 symmetrical wide.

---

### Why it works
A binary choice is the lowest-effort engagement there is — one tap or one word. Routing "save left" to a **like** and "save right" to a **comment** doubles the signal
from a single viewer and makes the counts visibly compete. The moral hook ("first
day" vs "little brother") gives people something to argue in the replies, and
**"pt 2 tomorrow"** converts the tension into a follow and a guaranteed return visit.
