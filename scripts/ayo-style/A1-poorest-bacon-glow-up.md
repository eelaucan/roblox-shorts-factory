# A1 — THE POOREST BACON GOT A GLOW UP 🥹✨

**Format:** Emotional underdog glow-up — the flagship AyoGaming beat.
**Game / Setting:** Catalog Avatar Creator spawn, or a quiet corner of Brookhaven.
Controlled lighting: overcast/blue for the sad half, warm/bright for the glow-up.
**Cast:** BACON (default avatar, no hats) · SLENDER + 2 friends (black hair, expensive
off-sale look) · HERO (soft warm colors, clearly kind, not flexy).
**Runtime:** ~33 s
**Title options:** "The poorest Bacon got a GLOW UP 🥹✨" · "Nobody believed in him… 😢✨" · "He had 0 Robux for 400 days 🥹"
**Hashtags:** #roblox #baconhair #robloxedit #glowup #roblox2026 #fyp

---

## 0:00 – 0:03 · HOOK
- **Visual:** Extreme close-up on BACON's face, tilted down, holding the sad/cry emote. Slight rain particles.
- **Text:** `nobody wanted to be his friend.`
- **Audio:** Sad piano starts on frame 1. No other sound.
- **Editing:** Hold the close-up completely still for 3 s — the only still shot in the video. Desaturate ~40%, soft vignette.

## 0:03 – 0:10 · THE BULLYING
- **Visual:** Pull to a wide. SLENDER and 2 friends circle BACON, hitting the `laugh` and `point` emotes. One does the `flex` emote showing an off-sale item in BACON's face. BACON sits down (`sit`).
- **Text:** Slender bubble — `why are you even playing 💀`
- **Audio:** Piano continues. A single soft *thud* when BACON sits — **not** an Oof.
- **Editing:** Quick zoom-punch onto each laughing face (0.5 s each). Keep BACON small and low in frame — visually powerless. Cool blue grade.

## 0:10 – 0:14 · ROCK BOTTOM
- **Visual:** BACON walks alone to a corner, back to camera. Rain heavier. The 3 bullies out of focus behind.
- **Text:** `day 400 of being invisible.`
- **Audio:** Piano swells to its first peak.
- **Editing:** Slow dolly-in on BACON's back. Darkest grade of the video. Hold 1 s longer than feels comfortable.

## 0:14 – 0:21 · SOMEONE STEPS IN
- **Visual:** HERO walks into frame, stops between BACON and the bullies, hits the `stop` / point emote straight at SLENDER. The bullies back up and leave.
- **Text:** HERO bubble — `leave him alone.`
- **Audio:** Piano shifts to a hopeful major chord. Rain starts to fade.
- **Editing:** First warm light of the video creeps in here. Cut to BACON's face looking up — the first time we see his eyes.

## 0:21 – 0:26 · THE GIFT
- **Visual:** HERO opens a trade window — a stack of cool items and hats flies across to BACON. BACON hits the `surprised` emote, hands to face.
- **Text:** `he didn't have to do that.`
- **Audio:** Piano gentle. A soft ascending *chime* as the items land.
- **Editing:** Show the trade window clearly for 1 s (proof it's real), then cut to BACON's reaction. Warm grade now fully in.

## 0:26 – 0:30 · GLOW UP
- **Visual:** **MUSIC SWITCH.** Spin transition → BACON re-emerges as a full cool avatar (glow, particle trail, a proper animation pack). He does the confident walk past the frozen SLENDERs. SLENDER hits the `shocked` emote.
- **Text:** `✨ GLOW UP ✨`
- **Audio:** Hard cut to an upbeat trending phonk/pop **drop** — land the switch exactly on the beat.
- **Editing:** Saturation +20%, speed 1.1×, cut on every beat. Whip-pan from old BACON to new BACON.

## 0:30 – 0:33 · OUTRO
- **Visual:** Freeze on the glow-up avatar mid-pose, then he turns and `wave`s at camera.
- **Text:** `Like & Subscribe if you love happy endings ❤️`
- **Audio:** Let the drop ride out.
- **Editing:** Freeze-frame, subtle zoom, grain. **Loop-bait:** final framing (cool avatar's face, centered) matches the 0:00 sad-Bacon close-up — same shot, opposite feeling.

---

### Why it works
The viewer is made *protective* of BACON in the first 3 seconds, so they stay to see
him okay. The **"leave him alone"** beat is the shareable moment — people send it
with "we need more players like this". The music switch at 0:26 is a scheduled
dopamine hit, and the CTA is bolted to the emotion (`if you love happy endings`)
instead of a generic "subscribe", which converts far better.
