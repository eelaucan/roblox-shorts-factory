# A2 — YOUR ROBLOX GLOW UP IF YOU… ✨👀

**Format:** "Your [X] if you…" — the comment-forcing engagement machine.
**Game / Setting:** Catalog Avatar Creator for the reveals; a spinning-wheel overlay
added in the edit.
**Runtime:** ~22 s
**Title options:** "Your Roblox glow up if you… ✨👀" · "Your 2026 avatar based on your vibe 🌸" · "Don't lie about what you got 👀"
**Hashtags:** #roblox #robloxavatar #glowup #robloxedit #fyp #roblox2026

---

## 0:00 – 0:03 · HOOK
- **Visual:** Six cool avatars flash past, one every 3 frames, then a spinning wheel snaps onto screen.
- **Text:** `your glow up if you're watching this 👀`
- **Audio:** Upbeat phonk, already mid-loop (no build).
- **Editing:** Cut on every avatar flash. Wheel drops in on a beat hit.

## 0:03 – 0:08 · THE RULES
- **Visual:** Wheel front and centre, 6 labelled wedges: `emo baddie 🖤` · `soft aesthetic 🌸` · `rich flex 💸` · `Y2K 🌈` · `slender 😈` · `preppy ✨`. It starts spinning fast.
- **Text:** `whatever you land on is your new avatar 👇`
- **Audio:** Rapid `tick-tick-tick` synced to the wedges passing the pointer.
- **Editing:** Keep the wheel dead-centre, everything else still — the eye locks to it.

## 0:08 – 0:15 · THE SPIN
- **Visual:** Wheel slows. Near-miss: pointer almost stops on `rich flex 💸`, then creeps one wedge more and clicks onto `soft aesthetic 🌸`. Hard cut to that full avatar spinning in Catalog Avatar Creator — pastel fit, soft hair, little wings.
- **Text:** `comment what you got 💬`
- **Audio:** `tick`s slow and space out; a small **airhorn** on the final click; sparkle chime on the avatar reveal.
- **Editing:** Slow-mo (0.4×) on the last two wedges for the near-miss tension. Zoom-punch on the avatar reveal.

## 0:15 – 0:22 · EVERYONE GETS ONE
- **Visual:** Rapid-fire — each of the 6 avatars full-body for ~1 s: baddie, rich flex, Y2K, slender, preppy, soft. Then back to the wheel, still.
- **Text:** `which one r u? 👀` then `like if you got baddie 🖤`
- **Audio:** One beat-synced cut per avatar; phonk pushes to a small drop on the last one.
- **Editing:** Each avatar = hard cut + tiny zoom. End on a freeze of the full wheel so a re-watcher can "spin again".

---

### Why it works
"Comment what you got" converts a passive viewer into a comment — and comment
velocity in the first hour is what the Shorts algorithm reads hardest. Splitting the
outro into **"which one r u"** (comment) plus **"like if you got baddie"** (like)
harvests both signals from one person. The near-miss on the wheel is a 2-second
story, and the 6-avatar montage makes people re-watch to "land on" a different one.
